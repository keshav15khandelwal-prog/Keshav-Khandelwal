Instructions

Choose a level and tap a column to drop your red disc. Connect four in a row (horizontally, vertically or diagonally) before the AI does. Level 1 uses plain minimax, Level 2 adds alpha-beta pruning, and Level 3 adds centre-first move ordering with a deeper search. Finish three games to unlock the next level. The Lab lets you watch each algorithm evaluate every column.

About

Approach: adversarial search.

Algorithms: depth-limited minimax, alpha-beta pruning, a heuristic evaluation function (windows of four cells plus a centre bonus), and centre-first move ordering.

This is a web port of the C program written for the AI and Soft Computing project.
